GUIDELINES FOR INTERACTING WITH KIDS IN CRISIS RESIDENTS

Thank you for spending your time volunteering with our children!

When you are volunteering with our residents, whether in the shelter or during a community outing, please observe the guidelines below. Most importantly, it is imperative that their identities be kept confidential at all times, even after you are no longer with them.

When interacting with the kids:

- Please keep the identities of our residents confidential

- While on community outings with the children, please do not make any special announcements or share with anyone that the children are from Kids in Crisis

- Please do not ask our residents questions about why they are staying at Kids in Crisis, how long they have been here, or any other details about their stay with us. Occasionally, as the child sees you on a regular basis and becomes more comfortable with you, they may talk openly about why they are at Kids in Crisis. These conversations must be initiated by the child and not the volunteer

- Please do not take any pictures or video, or allow residents to use your cell phones

- Please also refrain from sharing or requesting personal information of any kind. We realize volunteers have the best of intentions in wanting to keep in touch with the children. In accordance with agency policy, do not share your address, phone number or e-mail addresses with the residents.

- Please maintain appropriate physical boundaries with the children: this includes physical horseplay, carrying or excessive touching/hugging

- Please lock up ALL valuables. Staff will direct you to where you can lock things up. The best policy is to bring as little as possible with you into the volunteer activity

- For the safety of our residents, we keep sharp objects locked up: If you need a knife or pair of scissors during an activity, ask a counselor. Please be mindful of where you leave sharp objects after you finish an activity and return them directly to one of the counselors so they can put it away. For example, when cooking, never leave knives in the sink

- Ask for help: If you are unsure how to appropriately handle a situation with the kids, please discreetly pull a staff member aside to ask for help.

ABOVE ALL HAVE A GREAT TIME!
